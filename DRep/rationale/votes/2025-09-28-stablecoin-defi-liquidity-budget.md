## 投票根拠

---

This proposal represents an important attempt to strengthen DeFi liquidity on Cardano. However, I have concerns about the sustainability of its governance design. In particular, the balance of responsibilities and burdens among stakeholders does not seem sufficiently addressed, which could undermine the long-term health of governance. For these reasons, I choose to abstain.

I appreciate the direction Tempo.Vote has taken in aiming to broaden participation in governance through mobile-friendly UI/UX and independent infrastructure. However, this proposal still lacks sufficient clarity regarding fund management, development structure, and objective indicators of outcomes, making it difficult to make a fully informed decision. While I hope to see improvements in the future, I will abstain from voting on this proposal at this time.
